"rte" is a tokyo style car driving demo on the japanese freeway (like the shuto expressway.) the road never stops or slows down, and is generated endlessly. the city is just a bunch of skyscrapers.

[write your own version without peeking.] don't read htmls like grok.html or mai-code-flash.html.

[the demo is a japanesque.] the demo will feature a generic japanese car on a generic japanese highway. this is not a simulator. the difference lies in symbols, signage and other stuff.

[not a game.] there are no controls. rte is like a screensaver, or a demo, rather than a playable game.

[rendering pipeline.] just use a basic three.js setup. the goal is to get the that japanese sports car driving on that highway. i will tweak the renderer myself, and add shading effects later.

[write one big script.] keep writing and don't stop.

[be smart.] your goal is to meet project goals, not to write beautiful code. if it works it works.

[use open source if it works.] if you can find code in car games or other drive demos that are open source, just use it.

[write in whatever difficulty.] the code does not have to "look sane." if the agent can maintain the code cheaply, that is a win. just make sure to;

[put config values at the top of the files.] make sure i can easily access generation constants and values, like at the top of the script.

[suspended highway.] the highway in the demo shutoespressway will always be elevated. don't worry about placing supports, we don't see them.

[roadway lighting.] make sure to add street lamps at regular intervals, with working spotlights.

[traffic signs.] we will worry about adding traffic signs later.

[toll control points.] add a small chance of a toll building that sits over the road where cars have to slow down.

[reuse objects.] to prevent fps spikes, place objects that share geometry and materials.

[use placeholder objects.] don't try to generate complex geometry, just use gray boxes or rectangles to represent signs or skyscrapers
